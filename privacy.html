Synapse Up

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version

  • Synapse Up works without an account. Everything you do in the app (your name, notes, flashcards, scores, streaks, Brain Island, Brain Tree and settings) is saved only on your phone.
  • We don't run ads, we don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or share your data.
  • On Android, the optional app guard uses the Accessibility service to see only the name of the app you open, so it can show the brain gate. It never reads your screen, typing or messages, and that app name never leaves your phone.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of your data.

Who we are

Synapse Up ("the app", "we", "us") is an app that asks for a few quick learning rounds before you open apps you chose to limit. If you have a question about this policy, email us at synapseup.app@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

The app saves the following in your phone's private app storage. We can't see it, and it isn't sent to us or anyone else:

  • The first name or nickname you type, your level (high school, college or adult) and the fields you pick.
  • Notes you paste or scan and the flashcards made from them, plus how well you know each card.
  • Your daily activity: rounds, gates passed, focus minutes, debates practiced, streaks, sparks, Brain Island flowers and Brain Tree progress.
  • Debate arguments you write in Debate Coach or Debate Duel on your phone.
  • Which apps you guard, how long each one unlocks for, your blocker schedules and settings such as sound.

To erase it, use Block › Settings › Reset all data on this phone, or delete the app.

The Android app guard (Accessibility service)

On Android, you can turn on an optional feature called Synapse Up app guard. It uses Android's Accessibility service, and it's only on if you turn it on yourself in your phone's Settings, after the app explains what it does and you tap Agree.

  • What it reads: only the package name of the app that comes to the front of your screen (for example, com.google.android.youtube).
  • Why: when that app is one you chose to guard and it isn't unlocked, Synapse Up shows the brain gate first. That is the app's main feature.
  • What it never does: it can't read what is on your screen (window content access is switched off), and it doesn't read your typing, passwords, messages or notifications. It doesn't tap, type or change settings for you.
  • Where the data goes: nowhere. The app name is compared with your guarded list on your phone and then forgotten. It is never stored, sent or shared.

You can turn the app guard off at any time in Settings › Accessibility › Synapse Up app guard.

The list of apps on your phone

On Android, Block › Pick another app shows the apps on your home screen so you can choose ones to guard. The list is read on your phone, only while that screen is open, and it is never sent anywhere. The app does not ask for permission to see every installed app.

The camera (Snap your notes)

On Android, Add notes › Snap your notes opens a camera inside the app so you can photograph a page of notes. The camera is on only while that screen is open, and only after you allow it. The text on the page is read on your phone by a text reader built into the app. The photo is never uploaded or shared, and it is deleted as soon as the text has been read. Only the text is kept, in your notes, on your phone.

Speaking instead of typing

Synapse Up doesn't use your microphone. If you tap the microphone on your phone's keyboard to speak your answers, your keyboard (for example Gboard or the iPhone keyboard) turns your voice into text under its own privacy policy, and Synapse Up only receives the text.

Sharing

Some screens have a Share button (your weekly brain report, a class deck, your Brain Tree friend code). It opens your phone's normal share menu, and you decide what to send and to whom. We don't see what you share.

Purchases

Synapse Up is free. Synapse Up Plus is an optional subscription. On Android you buy it, change it and cancel it in Google Play, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google handles the payment: we never see your card or bank details, your name or your email. Google Play tells the app only which plan you have and whether it's active, and the app keeps that on your phone. Nothing about your purchase is sent to us.

Online debate challenges and friends' trees

Online features are optional. Nothing is sent to a server until you choose to go online by picking a handle, either in Debate Coach › Online challenges or in My Brain Tree › Friends' trees. Your notes, learning progress and blocked apps never leave your phone, and your real name doesn't either unless you save your account to Google (see below).

If you go online, our server keeps:

A random guest ID (no email, phone number or password)To keep your handle, rating and tree friends yours
Only if you tap Save with Google or Continue with Google: your Gmail address, Google account ID and the name on your Google account, from Google Sign-InSo you can get your online account back on a new phone. Other players never see them, and we never see your Google password
The handle you choose, an optional nickname, and your level (high school, college or adult)So others can challenge or add you, and to match you only with people at your level. High school players only meet other high school players

Blocks and reports you make about another playerTo keep players safe

This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, only for running these features. We don't sell it, share it, or use it for ads. You can delete your online account and all of this data, including your Google sign-in, at any time in the app (👤 on the Room › Manage online account › Delete my online account), or by emailing us.

Children

Synapse Up is made for high school students, college students and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app works without an account. If you choose to go online, we only receive the handle and game data listed above, plus your Gmail address and Google name only if you choose to save your account to Google. We never receive your contacts.

Security

Your data is kept in your phone's private storage for this app, which other apps can't read. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing on our side to be leaked.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without giving your real name: any nickname works, or leave it blank.
  • Turn the Android app guard on or off at any time.
  • Erase everything with Reset all data on this phone, or by deleting the app.
  • Wherever you live, you can email us with any privacy question or request. We'll answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app, and tell you in the app when the change matters to you.
